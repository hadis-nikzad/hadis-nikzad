# Hadis Nikzad

**Hydrological Modelling | Remote Sensing | Flood Risk**

I hold an MSc in Civil Engineering for Risk Mitigation from Politecnico di Milano (2026). My academic interests focus on hydrological processes, satellite remote sensing, and the use of observational data to improve rainfall–runoff modelling.

## Research Background

My master's thesis investigated the use of satellite-derived soil moisture data to characterise antecedent wetness conditions in the SCS Curve Number method.

The study involved analysing 213 rainfall–runoff events across nine catchments in northern Italy, examining the relationships between Curve Number variability, antecedent soil moisture, precipitation, and temperature.

I am currently working with my academic supervisors on a manuscript based on this research.

## Selected Academic Projects

* **Hydrological Modelling and Flood Risk Assessment:** Rainfall–runoff simulation, watershed delineation, IDF/DDF analysis, and flood hazard mapping.
* **Adda River Flood Risk Evaluation:** 1D and 2D hydraulic modelling using HEC-RAS and River2D.
* **Avalanche Hazard Mapping:** GIS-based hazard assessment and spatial analysis in the Italian Alps.
